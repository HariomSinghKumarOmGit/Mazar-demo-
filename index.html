Welcome To Mazaar Restaurant

Mazaar Restaurant brings you the true soul of Afghanistan right here in the heart of New Delhi. Located at I-78, Lajpat Nagar 2, we offer an immersive Afghani dining experience where every dish tells the story of ancient Central Asian culture and tradition.

Our chefs craft every meal using authentic Afghani spices and time-honoured recipes — from slow-chargrilled kebabs and hearty Afghani Pulao to the iconic Mutton Chopan Kabab, fresh Afghani Naan, and the sweet delight of Sheer Yakh. We also serve North Indian, Mughlai, Lebanese, and Pizza to make every visit memorable.

Whether you seek a casual lunch or a special dinner, Mazaar promises a warm atmosphere and real Afghani taste. Opens daily at 12 noon. Average cost for two: ₹1,000.
